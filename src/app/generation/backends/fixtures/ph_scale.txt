You have seen this strip. Zero is the strongest
acid, seven is neutral, fourteen the strongest alkali.
So one is more acid than two. But
how much more? The strip does not say.
It looks like a made up ranking.
So stop reading the numbers, and look inside the liquid.
This is the acid at pH one. Every red dot is one acid particle.
In one beaker of it, a hundred.
Now take one tenth of that liquid.
Put it in a fresh beaker and top it up with water.
Same size beaker. But only ten particles left inside. That is pH two.
Do it again. One tenth of this one, topped up with water.
Now one particle. pH three.
Each step along the scale is the same ten times.
pH one to pH three is two steps, so a hundred times fewer.
Nothing here was ranked. Something was counted.
One step down the scale is ten times more acid.
